Privacy Policy

Smart Closet · Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Smart Closet ("the app", "we") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Smart Closet is a wardrobe management app that lets you catalog your clothing, get AI-assisted styling suggestions, and optionally connect with a personal stylist or friends and swap pieces of clothing with them.

How we use AI features

The first time a photo would be sent, SmartCloset asks for your permission, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy → AI photo analysis. If you say no, nothing is sent and you can add items by entering the details yourself. The one exception is the safety check on photos you list for community swaps, which you agree to separately (see Clothing swaps below). When AI analysis is on and you photograph or search with a clothing item, the photo may be sent to third-party AI providers to identify its category, color, brand, and material, and to power reverse-image and text-based product search:

  • Google Cloud Vision API — image labeling and web/reverse-image search, and, only if you use color analysis, analysing a selfie you choose in order to estimate skin tone. It also reads the text on a clothing tag you photograph, and checks photos you list for community swaps for unsafe content and people (see Clothing swaps below); the swap check happens even if you turned off AI photo analysis
  • OpenAI (GPT-4 Vision) — detailed garment identification from photos
  • Brave Search API — text-based product search results
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast for your approximate location (no photos or personal details are sent)

These providers process the image or query to return results to the app; we don't control their retention practices beyond what's stated in their own privacy policies. We do not sell your photos or personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

If you scan a clothing tag, the photo of the tag is sent to Google Cloud Vision to read its text. The photo is not kept.

Shared product database (optional)

This is off unless you turn it on in Settings → Community → Share product details. When it is on, each time you add an item, its product details (name, category, brand, store, color, material, the price you paid and the retail price, and a product link if you picked one) are added to a database that other SmartCloset members can search when they add a similar item. Other members see these product details without your name or email.

What is never shared this way: your photos, your name, your email address, your wardrobe as a whole, your outfits, or your style and body profile.

Internally the entries are stored with your account ID so that you stay in control: you can turn sharing off at any time, choose to remove everything you have already shared, and deleting your account removes it too. The shared database is not sold and is not used for advertising. Items you add while sharing is off are used only on your own account.

Quality ratings and brand insights (optional)

You can rate items you own and brands you have bought from. Your ratings are private to your account by default, and a rating of an item is deleted when you delete the item. A private note you add to a rating is just for you.

Brand insights are a separate, optional step, and your version of the app may not offer them yet. Where it does, you can choose to add your ratings (Settings → Community → Add my ratings to brand insights, off by default, and only if you are 18 or older). Your scores and the words you chose (such as "soft" or "runs small") are then combined with other members' ratings of the same brand. They are counted with the brand, the type of item and, for items whose fabric you scanned from the tag, the main fabric. They are never shown with your name, email, photos, notes or closet, and nothing you write is shown to anyone else. Your private notes are never used.

A score is shown to other members only when at least 10 different members have contributed to it and at least 10 of them have had their account for more than a month; a finer breakdown (such as one fabric or the fit split) needs at least 10 members as well. Only accounts that are at least a week old, have a confirmed email address and have worn the piece they rated (for a rating of a brand, something from that brand) are counted. Member counts are shown as ranges and scores are rounded. Scores are refreshed about once a day.

You can stop at any time. Turning the setting off, or choosing Remove what I've added, takes your ratings out of the scores right away; scores already shown on other people's phones can take up to 24 hours to update. Deleting a rating, an item or your account also removes it. We cannot take back what another member has already seen.

Scores are members' opinions and are not verified. We do not sell this data and we do not share it with brands or retailers.

Clothing swaps (optional)

Swaps let you trade a piece of clothing with another member, item for item. SmartCloset never handles money and is not a party to a swap. Swaps with people who are not your friends (community swaps) are opened separately and may not be available yet.

Who can see what. A piece appears on the swap board only after you open it to swap, and only to the audience you choose: your accepted friends, or, if community swaps are open and you have joined, other members who have joined. They see its photo, name, brand, size, condition, materials, care instructions, country of origin, season and your short note. In a swap they never see what you paid, the notes you keep on the piece, how often you wear it, the text of its label, your ratings, your wishlist, your email address or your location. Friends see your usual name; community members see only the alias you choose.

Photos. When you open a piece to swap, we store a copy of its photo, with location and camera details removed, in a separate area used only for swaps. It is shown through links that stop working after ten minutes. If you list a piece for the community, the photo is also checked for unsafe content and people by Google Cloud Vision before it appears, and you agree to this check when you join. Photos you list for friends only are never sent to Google.

Messages. Messages in a swap are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. We read them only if someone reports them or to keep the service running. Please keep your address and phone number out of messages until you trust the person.

Age. Community swaps are for people 18 and over. We store the time you confirmed this, not your date of birth.

Working with a stylist (optional)

You can work with a stylist you choose. A stylist writes looks (styling suggestions) for you, either of you can propose a session (an appointment), and the two of you can exchange messages. You connect only through a one-time invite code that the stylist gives you; stylists are not listed anywhere in the app. Your version of the app may not offer this yet.

Independent professionals. Stylists are independent professionals. SmartCloset does not check or vouch for them, does not guarantee their advice or services, and is not a party to anything you arrange with a stylist. SmartCloset never takes payment, adds fees or holds money: any fee is agreed directly between you and your stylist, outside the app.

Who can see what. Connecting shares nothing. A stylist sees the name you choose to show them, never your account name or email address. They see your closet only if you switch that on for that connection, for all your pieces (including pieces you add while sharing is on) or only for the pieces you pick, and never your wishlist. For each piece you share they see its cover photo, name, category, subcategory, color, brand, season, pattern, material, materials, size and condition. They never see what you paid or the retail price, your notes, tags or label text, how often you wear things, your outfits, your style, body or skin-tone profile, your face photo, your location, your email address or phone number, your friends or your swaps. A session shows the other person only the time on the proposer's clock and its offset from UTC (for example UTC+1): that is a time, not a place, and no city or region is ever shown. A look the stylist writes keeps the name and category of each piece they put in it, and nothing else about the piece, until the look or the connection is deleted. Photos are sent to the stylist without location or camera details, and a photo can show more than the clothes, so please don't share pieces whose photos show people, especially children, or rooms and other private details. A stylist's profile (display name, headline, bio, services, languages and the rates the stylist wrote) is visible only to someone who holds one of that stylist's invite codes or is connected to them. Nothing in this feature is sent to an AI service, and a stylist cannot start one on your pieces.

Your control. You can switch closet sharing off at any time, from the connection's page under Home → Stylists, and the stylist loses access straight away. Turning it off sends your stylist no message; they simply see that your closet is not shared. Sharing also ends by itself after 90 days unless you renew it, and when either of you ends the connection. We can't take back what a stylist has already seen, which includes the names and categories of the pieces in looks they wrote: those stay in the look when you switch sharing off. The terms forbid copying it, passing it on, using it to train any model or keeping it after the relationship ends, but we cannot stop a screenshot. You can see when your closet was last viewed and how many closet pages the stylist looked at in the last 30 days, and your stylist can see whether and when you opened a look. Either of you can end a connection, report the other or block them at any time.

Messages and sessions. Messages, looks and sessions are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted. Messages are text only. We read them only if someone reports them or to keep the service running. If you rate a look, comment on it or say you wore it, your stylist sees that. Please keep your address and phone number out of messages until you trust the person, and never pay by gift card, cryptocurrency or wire transfer.

Age. Stylists and clients must be 18 or over. We store the time you confirmed this, not your date of birth.

Where your data is stored

Your account data, wardrobe items, and photos are stored using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure), with row-level security so only you can access your own data, except what you choose to share. If you add a friend and they accept, your wardrobe becomes visible to that specific friend (read-only), and you can remove that access at any time. In the app, your photos are shown to you and to friends you have accepted and, for pieces you open to swap, to the audience you chose under Clothing swaps. A photo's web address is not a password, so please don't share links to your photos. A stylist can see a client's closet pieces only while the client has switched sharing on; each photo is fetched through our server, which checks that sharing is still on and removes location and camera details before sending it.

Your ratings and notes are never visible to friends. By notes we mean the private note you can add when you rate a piece.

Community & safety

You can report or block another member from their friend request or closet, or from a swap listing or conversation. If you work with a stylist, stylists and clients can also report or block each other from a profile (including before connecting), a message, a look or a session. A report stores your account ID, the reported account's ID, where you saw them, the reason you chose, and any details you add (up to 500 characters). A report about a swap listing or conversation also keeps a copy of that listing or of the last 30 messages, so we can act on it. A report about a stylist or a client keeps a copy of the profile, of the last 30 messages, or of the look or session you reported, and a note of whether you and that person have worked together. Reports are used only to review and enforce our community guidelines, are visible only to us, and are never shown to the person you reported. Blocking is private: the person you block isn't told, only you can see your list of blocked people, and blocking ends any friend request, friendship or stylist connection between you. A block you make in Friends, Swaps or Stylists applies to all three. Reports and blocks are deleted along with either account.

Where brand scores are shown, you can report one that looks wrong or manipulated from the brand page. That report stores your account ID, the brand and the reason you chose, is read only by us, and is deleted with your account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account and all associated data (wardrobe items, outfits, ratings, style profile, friend connections, swap listings, offers and messages, and, if you used the stylist features, your stylist profile, invites, connections, shared-closet settings, looks, sessions and messages) from our systems. Copies in routine backups are overwritten on their normal schedule. This cannot be undone.
  • Your ratings — Settings → Community lets you delete all your ratings and, where brand insights are available, stop adding your ratings or remove what you added. Settings → Export Data includes your ratings.
  • Label scans — you can edit the care instructions and country of origin of any item when you edit it, and clear the stored tag text on the item's page (Label, then Tag text, then Clear tag text).
  • Your swap data — Settings → Export Data includes your swap listings, your offers and your own messages, never the other person's.
  • Your stylist data — from a connection's page you can, at any time, end the connection and report or block the other person, and, if you are the client, switch closet sharing off. Settings → Export Data includes your stylist profile, your invites, your connections with their looks, sessions and sharing history, and your own messages, never the other person's messages.
  • Location, camera, and photo access — controlled entirely through iOS Settings; the app functions without location access (weather suggestions are simply omitted).
  • Guest mode — you can use core wardrobe features without creating an account; that data stays on your device only.

Children's privacy

Smart Closet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Community swaps are for people 18 and over. Adding ratings to brand insights is for people 18 or older. Working with a stylist is for people 18 and over.
