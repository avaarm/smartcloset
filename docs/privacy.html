Privacy Policy

Smart Closet · Last updated September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Smart Closet ("the app", "we") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Smart Closet is a wardrobe management app that lets you catalog your clothing, get AI-assisted styling suggestions, and optionally connect with a personal stylist or friends.

How we use AI features

When you photograph a clothing item, the photo may be sent to third-party AI providers to identify its category, color, brand, and material, and to power reverse-image and text-based product search:

  • Google Cloud Vision API — image labeling and web/reverse-image search
  • OpenAI (GPT-4 Vision) — detailed garment identification from photos
  • Brave Search API — text-based product search results

These providers process the image or query to return results to the app; we don't control their retention practices beyond what's stated in their own privacy policies. We do not sell your photos or personal data to anyone, for any purpose.

Where your data is stored

Your account data, wardrobe items, and photos are stored using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure), with row-level security so only you can access your own data — with one exception: if you add a friend and they accept, your wardrobe becomes visible to that specific friend (read-only), and you can remove that access at any time.

Your choices

  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account and all associated data (wardrobe items, outfits, style profile, friend connections) from our systems immediately. This cannot be undone.
  • Location, camera, and photo access — controlled entirely through iOS Settings; the app functions without location access (weather suggestions are simply omitted).
  • Guest mode — you can use core wardrobe features without creating an account; that data stays on your device only.

Children's privacy

Smart Closet is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
